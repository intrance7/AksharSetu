## 1. Overview & Nature of the Platform

AksharSetu is a peer-to-peer community platform designed to bridge knowledge by facilitating:

**AksharSetu acts as an intermediary marketplace and communication platform.** Unless explicitly stated, AksharSetu is not a party to any transaction between buyers, sellers, donors, or recipients, and does not own, inspect, or hold custody of the books listed.

## 3. Listing & Transaction Rules

### 3.1 Book Listings
- **Condition Transparency**: Sellers/donors must provide honest descriptions and accurate photos (front cover, spine, pages with markings) of the book's physical state.
- **Authenticity**: Only legitimate physical books may be listed. Photocopied, pirated, or counterfeit editions are strictly prohibited.
- **Prohibited Items**: Digital files (PDFs, eBooks), test bank answers, pornographic/hate material, or non-book merchandise are not allowed.

### 3.2 Pricing & Payments
- **Sales**: Sellers determine their own listing prices. Transactions conducted locally or via integrated payments must be honored as agreed.
- **Donations ("Vidya Daan")**: Books listed as free/donation may not have hidden costs attached. If shipping is required, the recipient covers only actual courier fees.
