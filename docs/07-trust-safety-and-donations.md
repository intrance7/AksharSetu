# AksharSetu - Trust, Safety, and Donations

Marketplaces live or die by user trust. AksharSetu integrates strict moderation, condition grading, and transparent donation tracking.

## 1. Trust and Safety Mechanisms
To combat fake listings, scams, and abuse, the platform will enforce:
- **Identity Verification**: Email and Phone verification for all transacting users. (Future: College ID verification).
- **Listing Moderation**: Automated image/text checks prior to publishing.
- **Reporting System**: Users can flag suspicious listings, messages, or other users. Admins monitor a dedicated Moderation Queue.
- **In-App Messaging**: Buyers and sellers communicate exclusively via AksharSetu's internal messaging to protect privacy and provide an audit trail for disputes.

## 2. Structured Book Condition System
Subjective grading (e.g., just saying "Good") leads to buyer disputes. Sellers must provide structured condition data:
- **Grades**: `NEW`, `LIKE_NEW`, `GOOD`, `FAIR`, `POOR`
- **Required Checkboxes**:
  - Pages missing? (Yes/No)
  - Writing inside? (Yes/No)
  - Highlighting? (Yes/No)
  - Cover damaged? (Yes/No)
  - Water damage? (Yes/No)

## 3. The Donation Engine & Transparency
Donations are a core differentiator. It transforms an ordinary marketplace into a community-driven platform.

- **Campaigns**: NGOs or schools can create targeted campaigns (e.g., "Help 500 Students: Need 200 Math Books").
- **Transparency & Tracking**: Donors can track the exact lifecycle of their donation:
  *Donated -> Collected -> Verified -> Sent to NGO -> Received -> Distributed*
- **Impact Dashboard**: Donors receive tangible feedback, e.g., "Your donation helped 3 students at 1 school."
