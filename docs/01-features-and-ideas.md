# AksharSetu - Features and Ideas

Building **AksharSetu**, a platform bridging people through knowledge via book resale and donations.

## Core Platform Features
- **User Profiles**: Distinct profiles with ratings, reviews, badges (for donations), and transaction history.
- **Hyperlocal Discovery**: Location-based filtering so users can find books in their own city/neighborhood. Enables free local pickups.
- **Bundle Listings**: Sell/donate an entire semester's worth of textbooks or a fiction series as a single package.
- **Wishlist Alerts**: Search for a book, and if unavailable, set an alert. Be notified instantly when someone lists it.
- **Inventory Management**: Easy upload process via barcode scanning (using ISBN) to auto-fill book details, author, and cover image.

## Building the Donation Community
- **The "Vidya Daan" Leaderboard**: Gamify the donation process by awarding digital profile badges ("Knowledge Giver", "Community Pillar") to frequent donors.
- **Story Attached**: Donors can attach a short note about why they loved the book, adding a human touch.
- **NGO Partnerships**: A dedicated portal for local schools or charities to request specific bulk donations directly from the community.

## Trust and Safety
- **Condition Transparency**: Require specific photos (spine, front cover, back, random inside page) to prevent disputes over condition.
- **Two-Way Ratings**: Rate buyers and sellers post-transaction.
- **Secure Messaging**: In-app chat to protect phone numbers and privacy. No external communication necessary.

## Selling vs. Donating Dynamics

| Feature | Selling | Donating |
|---------|---------|----------|
| **Primary Motivation** | Recovering costs | Decluttering, helping others |
| **Platform Role** | Facilitating secure chat & payment | Maximizing reach and speed |
| **User Reward** | Monetary return | Profile badges, community goodwill |

## Delivery Logistics & Monetization
- **Local Pickup**: Preferred method. Safe meetup spots recommended by the app.
- **Third-Party Logistics (3PL)**: Integration with local courier services (e.g., Delhivery, Dunzo, Shiprocket) where the buyer bears shipping costs at checkout.
- **Monetization**: Featured listings (small fee to boost a book), optional "Premium" badges for sellers, ads (if needed later).
