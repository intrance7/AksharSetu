# AksharSetu - Community & Safety Guidelines

AksharSetu is built on trust, respect, and a shared love for learning. These guidelines outline expectations for all members of our community.

---

## 1. Book Condition Standards (Grading Scale)

To avoid disputes, all sellers and donors must accurately grade their books according to the standard scale:

| Condition | Description | Required Photos |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **New** | Brand new, unread, no marks, crisp cover and spine. | Front & back cover. |
| **Like New** | Minimal shelf wear, no writing, tight binding, pages clean. | Front, back, and spine. |
| **Good** | Gently used, slight cover creases, minimal highlighting/notes. | Front, back, spine, sample page. |
| **Acceptable** | Noticeable wear, heavier highlighting, intact pages and readable. | Front, back, spine, damaged areas. |

---

## 2. In-Person Meetup Safety Guidelines

When opting for free local pickup:
1. **Meet in Public**: Choose well-populated, public areas such as college campuses, coffee shops, libraries, or metro stations.
2. **Daylight Hours**: Schedule meetups during daytime or well-lit business hours.
3. **Inspect First**: Check the book condition to confirm it matches the listing before completing payment or transfer.
4. **Keep Communication on Platform**: Avoid sharing personal phone numbers or home addresses whenever possible.

---

## 3. "Vidya Daan" Donation Ethics

- **Authentic Intent**: Do not list unusable, damaged-beyond-reading, or severely defective books as donations.
- **Fair Allocation**: Priority should be given to students, learners, and verified community libraries in need.
- **No Hidden Fees**: Donations must be 100% free; only genuine third-party shipping costs may be requested if handoff isn't in-person.

---

## 4. Prohibited Content & Behavior

- **Counterfeit / Pirated Materials**: Photocopied textbooks or unauthorized reprints are strictly forbidden.
- **Harassment & Hate Speech**: Zero tolerance for offensive behavior in listings, messages, or reviews.
- **Scams & Off-Platform Solicitation**: Any attempt to bypass platform safety mechanisms or conduct fraudulent transactions will result in a permanent ban.

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

If you encounter a suspicious listing, counterfeit book, or fraudulent user, click **"Report Listing"** or email `safety@aksharsetu.org`.
