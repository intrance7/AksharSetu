# AksharSetu - Privacy Policy

*Last Updated: September 2026*

At **AksharSetu**, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how your data is collected, used, and shared.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Data**: Name, email address, phone number (for verification), profile photo, and bio.
- **Listing Data**: Book titles, authors, ISBNs, descriptions, pricing, and uploaded condition photos.
- **Location Data**: City, neighborhood, or postal code provided for hyperlocal discovery (exact street addresses are never made publicly visible).
- **Communication Data**: Messages exchanged via the in-app chat for coordinating pickups/deliveries.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Device & Usage Data**: IP address, browser type, device information, and interaction logs.
- **Cookies & Local Storage**: Session management, user preferences, and analytics tokens.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
1. Enable hyperlocal discovery of books in your vicinity.
2. Facilitate secure communication between buyers, sellers, and donors.
3. Manage wishlist notifications and book alerts.
4. Calculate user reputation, badges ("Vidya Daan"), and leaderboard rankings.
5. Prevent fraud, spam, and counterfeit book listings.

---

## 3. How We Share Information

- **Public Profile & Listings**: Your username, public badge achievements, ratings, city/neighborhood, and active listings are visible to other users.
- **Partner NGOs**: When you participate in bulk donation campaigns, your contact details may be shared with verified NGO coordinators with your explicit consent.
- **Third-Party Logistics (3PL)**: If shipping via integrated courier services, delivery name and address are shared with the logistics partner solely for order fulfillment.
- **We Never Sell Your Data**: AksharSetu does not sell personal user data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

---

## 4. Data Security & Retention

- We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to secure data in transit and at rest.
- User data is retained as long as your account remains active, or as required by law.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to:
- Access, review, and update your profile information in account settings.
- Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
- Opt out of non-essential email notifications and marketing alerts.

---

## 6. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries or data requests, contact our Data Protection Officer at `privacy@aksharsetu.org`.
